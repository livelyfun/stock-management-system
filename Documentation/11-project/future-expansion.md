# Future Expansion

The architecture should leave room for:

- Barcode / QR
- Multiple branches
- Vehicle management
- Route planning
- Mobile/PWA delivery interface
- Digital signature
- WhatsApp / SMS payment reminders
- Customer portal
- Supplier management
- Purchasing
- Production management
- Warehouse transfers
- Container tracking
- Expenses
- Payroll
- Tax/VAT accounting
- Profit/loss reporting
- Bank reconciliation

---

## Already Inside the MVP

Multiple warehouses are not a future expansion. They are inside the MVP,
with inventory scoped to a warehouse.

What remains future work on warehouses is:

- Warehouse transfers
- Warehouse-scoped pricing
- Warehouse-scoped authorization
- Container tracking

---

## Expansion Principle

Future features must integrate with the existing transaction, authorization, audit, and database integrity models.

New features must not bypass existing business controls.
