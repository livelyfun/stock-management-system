# Role-Based Web Experience

## Super Admin

Full system administration and authorized business operations.

---

## Admin

Business operations and management.

---

## Manager

Operations, inventory, and reports.

---

## Accountant

Access to:

- Sales records
- Invoices
- Payments
- Receipts
- Customer credit
- Customer ledger
- Financial reports

---

## Inventory Staff

Access to:

- Products
- Stock
- Stock movements
- Returns
- Inventory reports

---

## Delivery Staff

Access to:

- Assigned deliveries
- Required customer information
- Delivery confirmation
- Collections
- Returns

---

## Viewer

Read-only dashboards and approved reports.

---

## Security Rule

Hiding a navigation item is not authorization.

Server-side authorization must independently protect every operation.
