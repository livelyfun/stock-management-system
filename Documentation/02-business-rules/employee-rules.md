# Employee Rules

## 1. Employee Association

Employees may be associated with:

- Deliveries
- Returns
- Collections

---

## 2. Employee Operational Activity

The system should track:

- Deliveries
- Returns
- Collections

---

## 3. Employee Financial Activity

Where required, the system may track:

- Cash collection
- Cash submission
- Salary advance
- Fuel advance
- Other approved advances
- Deductions
- Adjustments

---

## 4. Employee Accountability

Management should be able to view:

- Deliveries by employee
- Products delivered
- Sales value handled
- Amount collected
- Outstanding collection
- Returns handled
- Employee advances / dues where required

---

## 5. Audit

Financial actions involving employee balances must be auditable.
